# RecipeBox

RecipeBox is a production-ready recipe management application that helps home cooks organize their culinary life. Built with modern software development practices, it provides a complete solution for managing recipes, planning meals, tracking nutrition, and creating shopping lists.

## Features

### Recipe Management
Users can create, store, and organize their recipe collection with detailed information including ingredients, instructions, and cooking times. Recipes can be easily edited or deleted, giving users full control over their personal cookbook.

### Meal Planning
Users can schedule meals on a calendar to organize their weekly meal prep and planning. The meal planner helps home cooks stay organized and reduce the stress of deciding what to cook each day.

### Shopping Lists
Users can generate shopping lists automatically from their planned meals, with ingredients intelligently combined to avoid duplicates. Shopping lists can be managed and checked off while at the store, streamlining the grocery shopping experience.

### Nutrition Tracking
Users can view detailed nutritional information for each recipe and track their daily intake to support their health goals. The nutrition tracker helps home cooks make informed decisions about their meals and monitor their dietary progress.

### Search
Users can quickly find recipes in their collection by searching for keywords in recipe names and descriptions. Advanced filters for cooking time and servings help home cooks find the perfect recipe for any occasion.
