RecipeBox needs to help users organize their recipes in a simple way. Each recipe should have a name, a list of ingredients with amounts, step-by-step instructions, and tags so people can find things easily later. Tags could be stuff like "vegetarian", "quick", "comfort food", whatever makes sense. The ingredients need to track quantities and units so we know exactly what we need when cooking.

For meal planning, users should be able to assign recipes to specific days of the week or month. Once they've planned out their meals, the app should generate a shopping list that pulls together all the ingredients from those recipes. The shopping list needs to be smart about grouping similar items together and aggregating amounts - like if three recipes all need onions, it should add up the total and show one line item. It should also handle unit conversion so if one recipe needs 2 cups of milk and another needs 1 pint, it can combine those intelligently.

Nutrition tracking is important too. Each recipe should show calories and macros - proteins, carbs, and fats. When someone assigns recipes to their meal plan, they should be able to see the nutritional breakdown for each day and maybe for the whole week. This helps people who are trying to hit specific calorie or macro targets without having to manually calculate everything themselves.
